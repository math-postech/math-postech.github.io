## Lecture Notes

Lecture notes are reconstructed from student in-class notes and the instructor's preparation notes. They are organized by lecture, following the existing course-page style: each item has a title, source scope, and short content summary.

### Foundations and Curves

- [Lecture 1: Functors, Schemes, and Projective Space](./notes/lecture-01-functors-schemes-projective-space.md) — Categories vs sets, representable functors, affine schemes as functors, Zariski topology, and projective space as a functor of lines.
- [Lecture 2: Divisors, Line Bundles, and Picard Groups](./notes/lecture-02-divisors-line-bundles-picard.md) — Curves, divisors, rational equivalence, line bundles on curves and on $\mathbb{P}^1$, divisor-line bundle correspondence, Picard group.
- [Lecture 3: Riemann–Roch and Weierstrass Equations](./notes/lecture-03-riemann-roch-weierstrass.md) — Riemann–Roch, canonical divisors, differentials on $\mathbb{P}^1$, genus-one curves, construction of $1,x,y$, and the Weierstrass equation.

### Elliptic Curve Structure

- [Lecture 4: Group Law on Elliptic Curves](./notes/lecture-04-group-law-on-elliptic-curves.md) — The identification $C\cong\operatorname{Pic}^0(C)$, point addition by tensor product of degree-zero line bundles, and chord-and-tangent geometry.
- [Lecture 5: Invariant Differentials](./notes/lecture-05-invariant-differentials.md) — The canonical differential $dx/(2y+a_1x+a_3)$, translation invariance, and uniqueness of global differentials.
- [Lecture 6: Isogenies, Hurwitz, and Degree](./notes/lecture-06-isogenies-hurwitz-degree.md) — Nonconstant origin-preserving morphisms, Hurwitz formula, unramified maps, multiplication-by-$n$, and $\deg[n]=n^2$.
- [Lecture 7: Theorem of the Cube and Line Bundles](./notes/lecture-07-theorem-of-cube-line-bundles.md) — Cube theorem, recurrence for $[n]^*\mathcal L$, symmetric line bundles, and the bridge to heights.

### Isogenies, Frobenius, and Tate Modules

- [Lecture 8: Isogenies and Group Structure](./notes/lecture-08-isogenies-group-structure.md) — Cube theorem applications, determinant line bundles, pushforward by isogenies, and why isogenies preserve addition.
- [Lecture 9: Dual Isogeny, Weil Pairing, and Endomorphisms](./notes/lecture-09-dual-isogeny-weil-pairing-endomorphisms.md) — Dual isogenies, positive definite pairing on homomorphisms, restrictions on $\operatorname{End}(E)$, and Weil pairing motivation.
- [Lecture 10: Finite Fields, Frobenius, and Point Counting](./notes/lecture-10-finite-fields-frobenius-point-counting.md) — Frobenius fixed points, $\#E(\mathbb F_q)=\deg(1-\operatorname{Frob}_q)$, Hasse bound, and zeta-function motivation.
- [Lecture 11: Tate Modules and the Weil Pairing](./notes/lecture-11-tate-modules-and-weil-pairing.md) — Inverse limits, $E[\ell^n]$, $T_\ell E\cong\mathbb Z_\ell^2$, finite-level Weil pairings, and the symplectic structure on Tate modules.
- [Lecture 12: The Hom Injection and the Tate Functor](./notes/lecture-12-hom-injection-and-tate-functor.md) — Proof that $\operatorname{Hom}(E_1,E_2)\otimes\mathbb Z_\ell$ injects into Tate-module homomorphisms; factorization through $[\ell^m]$ and divisibility.
- [Lecture 13: Trace, Determinant, Hasse Bound, and Supersingular Behavior](./notes/lecture-13-trace-determinant-hasse-supersingular.md) — Trace/determinant on $T_\ell E$, characteristic polynomial, Hasse bound, Frobenius eigenvalues, and ordinary vs supersingular $p$-torsion.
