Lecture 10: Finite Fields, Frobenius, and Point Counting ​

Student notes: begins around global PDF p. 98
Prep source: lecture11.md
Theme: Frobenius is the endomorphism whose trace controls point counts.

Overview ​

Let E/Fq be an elliptic curve. The arithmetic question is:

#E(Fq)=?

The geometric answer is to use Frobenius:

Frobq:E→E,(x,y)↦(xq,yq).

Then

E(Fq)=ker⁡(1−Frobq).

1. Frobenius Fixed Points ​

Definition

The q-power Frobenius endomorphism is

Frobq(P)=P(q)

on coordinates.

Proposition

A geometric point P∈E(F―q) lies in E(Fq) if and only if

Frobq(P)=P.

Proof. A coordinate lies in Fq exactly when it is fixed by a↦aq. Applying this to the coordinates of P gives the claim. ◻

Corollary

E(Fq)=ker⁡(1−Frobq).

2. Counting by Degree ​

Proposition

The endomorphism 1−Frobq is separable, and

#E(Fq)=deg⁡(1−Frobq).

Proof idea. The kernel of 1−Frobq is exactly the rational-point group. The differential of Frobenius is zero in characteristic p, while the differential of the identity is the identity. Hence the differential of 1−Frobq is nonzero, so the map is separable. For a separable isogeny, the number of geometric kernel points equals the degree. ◻

Remark

This is the first major point-counting conversion:

count points⇝compute degree of an endomorphism.

3. Trace of an Endomorphism ​

Definition

For an endomorphism ϕ of an elliptic curve, define its trace by

tr(ϕ)=1+deg⁡ϕ−deg⁡(1−ϕ).

Proposition

For Frobenius,

#E(Fq)=q+1−tr(Frobq).

Proof. Frobenius has degree q. By definition,

tr(Frobq)=1+q−deg⁡(1−Frobq).

Using deg⁡(1−Frobq)=#E(Fq) gives the formula. ◻

Remark

Preparation-note motivation

This is the elliptic-curve shadow of the Lefschetz trace formula: fixed points are counted by traces of an operator.

4. Hasse Bound ​

Theorem

Let

aq=tr(Frobq).

Then

|aq|≤2q.

Equivalently,

|#E(Fq)−(q+1)|≤2q.

Proof idea. The degree pairing on End(E) is positive definite. Applying the Cauchy inequality to 1 and Frobq gives

tr(Frobq)2≤4deg⁡(Frobq)=4q.

Taking square roots gives the bound. ◻

Attention

The proof uses the positivity of degree. This is why the previous lecture’s endomorphism pairing is not optional background.

5. Zeta Function Motivation ​

Definition

The zeta function of E/Fq is

Z(E/Fq,t)=exp⁡(∑n≥1#E(Fqn)tnn).

Proposition

If α,β are the Frobenius eigenvalues, then

#E(Fqn)=qn+1−αn−βn.

Explanation. Rational points over Fqn are fixed points of Frobqn. The trace of Frobqn is αn+βn, while its degree is qn.

TIP

Lecture takeaway

Frobenius packages all finite-field point counts. To make “eigenvalues of Frobenius” rigorous, the course next introduces Tate modules.
